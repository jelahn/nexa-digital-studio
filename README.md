# NEXA Digital Studio website

A responsive, single-page website for NEXA Digital Studio, a digital content studio serving home improvement contractors.

## Preview
Open `index.html` in a browser.

## Current configuration
- Customer inquiry email: `Gogreentechs@gmail.com`
- Pricing is displayed as a total for a three-month engagement: Essential $149, Growth $299, Expanded $499. These are three-month totals, not monthly charges.
- The contact form prepares an email in the visitor's default email app using a `mailto:` link. It does not submit directly to a CRM or send in the background; the visitor must send the prepared email.

## Before publishing commercially
1. Test the contact form on desktop and mobile with an email app configured.
2. If you want inquiries to submit without opening the visitor's email app, connect a hosted form endpoint or CRM.
3. Confirm business name/domain availability; this is a proposed brand concept, not trademark clearance.
4. Confirm package scope, delivery schedule, revisions, cancellation/refund terms, privacy terms, and any applicable legal requirements in a written agreement.
5. Add real portfolio examples only with permission. Do not claim results or testimonials that have not been earned.
